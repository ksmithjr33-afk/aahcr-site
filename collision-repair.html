Collision repair questions

Do I have to use the shop my insurance recommends?
No. In Texas you choose where your car is repaired. A carrier's preferred shop is a suggestion, not a requirement.
How long will my car be in the shop?
Cosmetic repairs often take a few days. Structural work depends on parts availability and insurance approval. We give you a realistic date at drop off and update you if it moves.
What about the deductible?
On an at fault claim your deductible is paid to the shop when you pick up the car. If you weren't at fault and the other driver's insurance is paying, there is usually no deductible.
Will the paint match?
We mix to your factory paint code, spray a test card, and blend into adjacent panels so there's no hard line where new paint meets old.
Do you work on electric vehicles?
Yes. Teslas and Cybertrucks come through the shop every week for both collision and wrap work.

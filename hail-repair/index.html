What paintless dent repair is

Hail leaves hundreds of small dents with the paint still intact. Instead of filling and repainting the panel, a PDR technician works each dent from behind with steel rods and from the front with glue tabs, watching the reflection in a striped light board until the panel reads flat again.

The factory paint stays on the car. No body filler, no color match problems, no repainted panels showing up on a vehicle history report. That matters for resale and it matters for lease returns.

The same technique fixes door dings and parking lot dents, with or without a claim.

Some hail damage is too deep or sits on an edge where PDR can't reach. We'll tell you that at the estimate, and we do conventional repair in the same shop, so you never get sent somewhere else.

Hail repair questions

Will a hail claim raise my rates?
Hail falls under comprehensive coverage, which covers things that happen to the car rather than things you did. Comprehensive claims are treated differently from at fault collision claims by most carriers. Your policy and your carrier decide the specifics, so check with your agent if you want it in writing.
What if my deductible is high?
Bring your claim paperwork and we'll walk you through what the estimate covers and what you'd owe before any work starts. On most comprehensive hail claims the customer ends up paying nothing out of pocket, but we'll tell you the number for your car, not a slogan.
How long does it take?
Light to moderate hail is usually two to four days once the claim is approved. Heavy hail with roof or hood replacement can run longer because of parts. Insurance approval is the part we can't control, so we start that the day you come in.
Is the repair under warranty?
Yes. Every repair comes with a lifetime warranty, in writing. Paintless dent repair doesn't involve paint or filler, so there's nothing to crack or fade.
Can I bring it in without a claim yet?
Yes. We'll inspect it under the light, photograph the damage and give you a written estimate you can send to your insurance company. If you want us to file for you, we do that too.

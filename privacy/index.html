Privacy policy

Last updated November 5, 2026

Advanced Auto Hail & Collision Repair LLC ("we", "us") operates this website and the shop at 15222 King Rd, Suite 1102, Frisco, TX 75036. This policy explains what we collect when you use the site or contact us, and what we do with it.

What we collect

  • Information you give us through the estimate form or by phone or text: name, phone number, email address, vehicle year, make and model, photos of damage, insurance claim details and anything you write in the message field.
  • Information your browser sends automatically: IP address, device and browser type, pages visited and referring site. We collect this through standard analytics and advertising tags, including Google and Meta.

How we use it

  • To respond to your estimate request, schedule your repair and keep you updated while your vehicle is in the shop.
  • To file and manage an insurance claim on your behalf when you ask us to.
  • To measure how people find and use the site, and to show relevant ads to people who have visited it.

Text messages

When you submit your phone number through our form or text us, you agree to receive text messages from us about your estimate, appointment and repair. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP at any time to opt out and HELP for help. Your consent to receive texts is not a condition of purchase. We do not sell or share your mobile phone number or your text message consent with third parties for their marketing.

Who we share it with

  • Your insurance company and adjuster, when you ask us to handle a claim.
  • Service providers that run our CRM, scheduling, email and text messaging on our behalf. They may only use your information to provide those services to us.
  • Law enforcement or regulators when the law requires it.

We do not sell your personal information.

Cookies and ads

The site uses cookies and similar tags from Google and Meta to measure traffic and ad performance. You can limit these through your browser settings and through Google and Meta's ad preference tools.

Keeping it secure

We keep your information on systems protected by access controls and encryption in transit. No system is perfectly secure, so we can't guarantee against every possible breach.

Your choices

You can ask us to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you, or to stop contacting you, by calling 469-237-1651 or visiting the shop. We'll keep records we need for warranty, insurance or legal reasons.

Children

This site is not directed at children under 13 and we don't knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If we change this policy we'll post the new version here with a new date.

Wrap questions

How long does a wrap last?
A quality cast film, installed properly and garaged, usually runs five to seven years in Texas sun. Matte and satin finishes need a little more care than gloss.
Does a wrap damage the paint?
No. Over factory paint in good condition a wrap protects it. Repainted or peeling surfaces are the exception, and we check for that before quoting.
How long is the car in the shop?
A full color change is typically three to five days. PPF front ends are one to two. Tint is same day.
Can you wrap a Cybertruck?
Yes, and we do. The flat stainless panels take film well and hide fingerprints and scuffs once wrapped.
Can I get a price without bringing it in?
Send the year, make, model and the finish you have in mind through the form and we'll quote it. Final pricing is confirmed when we see the paint.
